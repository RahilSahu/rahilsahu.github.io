A1 process & workflow automation with AI

Automate the pipeline, keep the human in the loop.

AI does the repetitive, high-volume work — ingesting documents, enumerating scope, drafting reports — and a person makes every decision that matters. Every pipeline below ends at a review gate: nothing goes to a client, a team, or the public without human approval.

grc / tprm

Security questionnaire automation

Incoming vendor questionnaires and client security assessments are ingested, parsed question by question, mapped against our ISO 27001 control set, and matched to the relevant evidence documents. Draft answers are assembled from those documents — grounded in actual evidence, never invented — and the completed draft lands with me for review before anything leaves the building.

Ingestquestionnaire intake
MapISO 27001 controls
Draftevidence-grounded
Human reviewapprove / edit
Send

Gate: answers are only as good as their evidence. Anything unanswerable from documents goes back as a named-owner question, not a guess.

offensive security

VAPT pipeline automation

Recon runs as a repeatable pipeline: subdomain enumeration, live-host probing, templated vulnerability scanning, and structured finding intake. The machine handles the breadth; the interesting part — manual exploitation, chaining, and business-logic flaws — stays human. Reports are drafted from validated findings, and retests are tracked until closure.

ProbeHTTPx · Nmap
ScanNuclei · Burp
Manual validationexploit · chain
Report · retestDefectDojo

Gate: scanner output is untrusted input until a human confirms exploitability. No scanner finding ships without validation.

devsecops

AppSec / DevSecOps automation

SAST, DAST and SCA run inside CI on every pull request so defects surface at code time, not audit time. Findings sync into DefectDojo, where they are deduplicated, assigned to owners, and tracked against remediation SLAs. Developers get PR-ready fixes; I get a single tracked workflow from detection to verified closure.

CI scanSonarQube · Snyk · CodeAnt.ai
PR findingsinline fixes
SyncDefectDojo
SLA trackingowners · retest

Gate: false-positive triage stays manual. A scanner does not get to burn developer trust on bad findings.

Phishing simulations run on a managed schedule with GoPhish — campaign setup, target lists, template rotation, and result dashboards — feeding straight into awareness training for the teams that clicked.

Operating principles

  • Human-in-the-loop. Every pipeline ends at a review gate. AI proposes; a person disposes.
  • Evidence-grounded. Drafts cite documents, controls and tooling. If it cannot be sourced, it is not written.
  • No autonomous sending. No email, post, answer or campaign leaves without explicit human approval.
  • Transparent about automation. Where it matters, recipients know a draft was machine-assisted.
  • Boring before clever. Automate the repeatable first; keep the novel, adversarial work human.
